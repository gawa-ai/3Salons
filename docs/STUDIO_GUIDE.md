# Studio guide (for Shahina and the artists)

Studio is the private area at `/signin`. Customers never see it.

## First sign-in
- **Shahina (owner):** open `/join`, enter your email, the invitation code you were given, and choose a password (10+ characters). The code works once, for that email only, and expires after 7 days.
- **Artists:** Shahina creates a code for each artist in **Team → Invite**. Open `/join` and do the same. If a code expires, Shahina makes a new one.
- Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page.

## What each person sees
- **Shahina** sees the whole salon. Use the selector at the top: **All professionals** or one artist.
- **Each artist** sees only their own bookings, clients, notes, enquiries and calendar. Nobody can open another artist's records.

## Everyday jobs
- **Needs confirming:** every online booking starts as *awaiting confirmation*. Open it and press **Confirm** or **Decline**. The customer sees the status on their private booking link.
- **Calendar:** day and week views. Add a manual booking for a walk-in or phone customer.
- **Availability:** set your working hours and add time off. Shahina also sets the salon opening hours.
- **Services:** check the duration of each service. Prices are changed by Shahina only.
- **Gallery:** upload photos of your own work, only with the client's permission.
- **Enquiries:** bridal and other enquiries addressed to you.

## Shahina's first-time setup (Studio → Settings and Availability)
1. Address, phone and WhatsApp, and the booking policy.
2. Salon opening hours, then tick *These are the confirmed opening hours*.
3. Each service's duration, then tick *details confirmed*.
4. Invite the three artists (Team → Invite).
5. Run the checks in `ACCEPTANCE.md`. Only after they pass, switch **Booking mode** to **Live**.

## Good to know
- Until Booking mode is **Live**, the site shows a "Preview site" banner and bookings are test bookings.
- Text messages (SMS/WhatsApp) are **not connected yet**, so nothing is sent to customers by text.
- Nothing is deleted when you cancel or decline; it is kept in the **Activity log** (owner only).
